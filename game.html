📖 How to Play

  1. Two players take turns rolling the dice.
  2. Move your token forward according to the dice number.
  3. If you land at the bottom of a ladder, climb up!
  4. If you land on a snake's head, slide down!
  5. You must land exactly on square 100 to win.
  6. The first player to reach square 100 wins!
